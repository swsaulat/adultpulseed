PulseED · Quick Guide

Reassessment Area Management System: keep one copy at the station

Live: adultpulseed.patientflowms.com
Training: adultpulseed.patientflowms.com/?training

1 · Sign in

  • Open adultpulseed.patientflowms.com and click Continue with Google with your own account.
  • The station locks after 15 min idle. Sign in again; nothing is lost.
  • Not on the staff list? Ask the charge nurse.

2 · Add a patient

  • Click + Add Patient. Copy the MRN from TrakCare, add name, chief complaint and CTAS.
  • Tick what the patient is waiting for: Vital Signs, Bed, Procedure / Treatment / Imaging (one or more).
  • Bed: enter the physician who booked it. The booking time is recorded for you.

Take them, document in TrakCare as usual, then press Done. Amber = due soon, red = overdue (chimes).

4 · Bed and procedure / imaging

  • Waiting for Bed shows who booked each bed, when, and how long the patient has waited.
  • When the bed is given, press Assign bed and type the bed. The patient leaves PulseED.
  • Procedure / treatment / imaging: set In Progress, then Done.

5 · Patient leaving

  • Bed assigned: the patient moved to an ED bed.
  • Discharged: pick the reason (Discharged home, DAMA, Absconded, LWBS, Transferred) or type another.
  • Entered in error: wrong patient or duplicate.
  • Everything open is closed at once. Mistake? Undo on the dashboard.

6 · Handover and reports

  • Shift Handover builds the SBAR from the live list. Copy or print it.
  • A shift report (one PDF) is emailed automatically at 06:00, 14:00 and 22:00.
  • Charge nurses and leaders also see Reports and the Audit Trail.

Training: practise before your first live shift

Open adultpulseed.patientflowms.com/?training. Made-up patients only, kept on that PC; nothing reaches the live register. Use any name to sign in. Try each task once:

  1. Add a CTAS 3 patient waiting for vital signs and a bed (booked by a physician).
  2. Press Done for an overdue vital signs patient.
  3. Move a procedure or imaging request to Done.
  4. On Waiting for Bed, assign a bed and see it under “Beds assigned”.
  5. Discharge a patient as DAMA, then undo it from the dashboard.
  6. Open Shift Handover and the Audit Trail and find your own actions.

Never enter real patients in training. The orange TRAINING bar shows you are in training.
